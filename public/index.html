How it works

  1. Retrieve

    The books are split into paragraph-based chunks of about 240 words that never cross a chapter boundary. Search is BM25, a standard keyword ranking formula.

  2. Answer

    With a model key, a model reads the top passages and gives a quote for every claim. Without one, the answer is the best matching sentences from the best passages.

  3. Check

    Every quote is searched for in the passage it cites. A quote that fails is shown as unverified, never dropped.
